Before leaving this running

Save or resolve any dirty active model yourself. The batch never saves your SKPs. Keep Windows awake and on power; screen locks, modal dialogs, native operations and disk limits can interrupt unattended work.

Cancel stops new jobs immediately and unwinds at a safe checkpoint. A noninterruptible publication may finish and be verified first. Pause finishes the active profile. After a restart, Load and reverify, then Resume. Successful export does not certify fidelity, performance or research acceptance.
